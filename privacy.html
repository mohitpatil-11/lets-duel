Let’s Duel – Privacy notice

Let’s Duel is a free browser game. It has no accounts, no ads and no chat, and it does not collect or sell personal data.

What is stored on your device

Your choices (weapon, map, difficulty, sound on/off) are saved in your browser’s local storage so the game remembers them. They never leave your device. You can clear them by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the game, and may log visits as any web host does.
  • Google Fonts, Tailwind CDN and unpkg deliver fonts and scripts, and can see your IP address when you load the page.
  • PeerJS cloud server and Metered (TURN relay) help online players connect, and see connection details such as IP addresses.

Online play

Online matches are peer-to-peer (WebRTC). The players in a match connect to each other, so they can technically see each other’s IP address. Only play online with people you trust, and do not post your room code publicly.

Children

The game collects no personal information. Younger players should use online mode only with a parent’s or guardian’s knowledge.

Whirlshot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Whirlshot is a free browser game. It has no accounts and no chat, and it does not currently show ads or use analytics. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Stored on your device

Your choices (name, weapon, map, difficulty, sound), coins and skins are saved in your browser’s local storage. They stay on your device. Clear them by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the game and may log visits like any web host.
  • Google Fonts, Tailwind CDN and unpkg deliver fonts and scripts and can see your IP address when the page loads.
  • PeerJS cloud server and Metered (relay) help online players connect and see connection details such as IP addresses.

Online play

Matches are peer-to-peer (WebRTC). The players in a match connect to each other, so they can technically see each other’s IP address. The name you enter is shown to the other players in your match. Do not use your real full name or personal details, and only play online with people you trust.

Children

The game does not ask for or collect personal information. Younger players should use online mode only with a parent’s or guardian’s knowledge.

If this changes

If we add ads, analytics or purchases, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
